Evaluate a listing from Zillow, Redfin or Realtor.com

  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:
    Evaluate listing
  2. Open a home's listing on Zillow, Redfin or Realtor.com.
  3. Click Evaluate listing in your bookmarks bar. This page opens with the price, beds, baths, size, year built, zip and HOA filled in.

It reads the page you're looking at, in your own browser; nothing is sent to this server until the evaluator opens. Listing pages change, so always check the numbers it fills in. On a phone, paste the listing text into Ask Claude instead.
